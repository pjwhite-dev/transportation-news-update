How this page works. A local newsroom checks agency, trade and search feeds around the clock. Each story is read and summarized by AI, and the summary links to the source. Quick summary means code has matched every name, number and date in our summary to the article. Summary double-checked means a second AI model, from a different family, has also confirmed that each sentence says what the article says. Neither is a check of the reporting itself. Minor, Notable and Significant are an AI estimate of a story's impact (government action, scale, how widely it is reported), not a judgment of the reporting. If a summary fails a check, only the headline and link are shown. Always read the source.
