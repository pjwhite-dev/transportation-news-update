How this page works. A local newsroom checks agency, trade and search feeds around the clock. Each story is read and summarized by AI, and the summary links to the source. Reports of the same event from several outlets are shown once, with the other outlets listed under it. ✓ means code has matched every name, number and date in our summary to the article. ✓✓ Double-checked means a second AI model, from a different family, has also confirmed that each sentence says what the article says. Neither is a check of the reporting itself. Minor, Notable and Significant are an AI estimate of a story's impact (government action, scale, how widely it is reported), not a judgment of the reporting. If a summary fails a check, only the headline and link are shown. Always read the source. U.S. focus (the default) leaves out stories from abroad with no U.S. government, company or place in them; Everything shows them too. Each story's Copy link gives an address that opens the page at that story. Search looks through the stories on this page (the last three days); Past days keeps every earlier day, searchable too. Times are Eastern; today's stories say how long ago they were published, and holding the pointer on a time shows the exact time.

Market quotes from Finnhub, delayed; not investment advice. Index tiles use the ETFs that track each index.
