# Incident 2026-10-23: no edition published

Status: proximate cause CONFIRMED; trigger of the Ollama outage PROBABLE (not proven).
Investigated 2026-10-24 (Phase 0, read-only until the cause was found).

## Confirmed cause
The Hermes "ATU bounded Tuesday-Friday build" cron job (profile `advtransport`, script
`hermes_daily_build.py`, schedule 04:15 ET) ran on time on 2026-10-23 and failed at 04:16:05 with
`Ollama structured generation failed after 2 attempts ... localhost:11434 ... WinError 10061
(connection actively refused)`. Ollama was not listening. The job has no restart/recovery step, so no
edition was built. The 09:30 "ATU Tuesday-Friday delivery" job then failed with `RuntimeError: Connection error`,
and the 09:25-12:55 "validated website publication" job ran "ok" because there was nothing to publish.
No alert was raised anywhere: Hermes delivery was `local` / bot-chat and the failure was never surfaced.

## What was ruled out
- No unpushed local commit (`origin/main..HEAD` empty); no OneDrive conflict copies in the repo.
- No reboot (last boot 2026-10-13 12:58), no shutdown/crash events (1074, 6005, 6006, 6008, 41 absent), no sleep/wake events,
  no python.exe or ollama.exe application-crash events in 10-22 18:00 to 10-23 12:00.
- No push race: the Hermes path never reached the push step.

## Probable trigger of the Ollama outage
Ollama stopped logging at 03:11:59 and was not running at 04:16, with no crash event. Microsoft Store updates (including OpenAI.Codex) ran
at 03:16 and the n8n node process restarted at 03:17. Most likely the Ollama process was terminated or not restarted by
the update activity. Not proven: the Ollama log rotated away before it could be checked beyond 03:11.
The fix is the same either way: Ollama runs as a boot service with a watchdog and the runner probes and restarts it (plan 7.4, 7.9).
